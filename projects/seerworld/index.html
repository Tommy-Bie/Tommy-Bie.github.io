Generative video world models

The world should keep evolving—even when unseen.

SeerWorld enables entities to remain active participants in a changing world, reason about off-screen events, and reveal plausible evolution when the camera returns.

01

The challenge

A coherent world is more than a remembered frame.

When the observer looks away, new events should still alter the world. A successful model must execute the switched event, preserve the subject and scene, and render a causally plausible change when the camera returns.

01

Look away

The camera leaves a visible subject and establishes an observation gap.

02

Change the world

A new event is introduced mid-rollout, without revealing the future response.

03

Return and reveal

The revisited subject must remain recognizable and show a plausible consequence.

02

The framework

A reasoning brain.
A visual world renderer.

SeerWorld separates world-state reasoning from rendering. A VLM continuously records what the renderer actually generated, then reasons about an appropriate subject state after an event is observed.

01

Persistent world memory

The brain records visible entities, scenes, object states, and realized changes after every segment.

02

Identity-Anchored Attention

Retrieved entity evidence receives stronger attention when a subject reappears.

03

Online rollout refinement

Stage 2 trains on model-generated predecessors to reduce the train–inference gap.

SeerWorld-Data provides segment-level supervision for event realization, long-range continuity, and behavioral change. SeerWorld-Bench tests the full event–revisit–reaction chain on screened, non-overlapping cases.
